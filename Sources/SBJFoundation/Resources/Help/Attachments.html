TITLE

Attachments associate files or embedded resources with the current item.

Managing Attachments

  • UI_add\ Use Add Attachment to choose one or more files.
  • UI_attachments\ Rename an attachment by editing its name. The filename extension is preserved.
  • UI_previewAttachment\ Preview a supported attachment without leaving the editor.
  • UI_remove\ Remove an attachment from the current item.

Removing an attachment removes its relationship to the item. Storage-specific cleanup is controlled by the attachment owner.
